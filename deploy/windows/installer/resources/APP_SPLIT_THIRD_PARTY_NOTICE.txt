IMPORTANT / EXPERIMENTAL
------------------------
Application split tunneling is disabled by default. As of July 2026 there is
an open Mullvad issue reporting nonpaged-pool/IRP growth with version 1.3.0.0
of mullvad-split-tunnel.sys under very network-heavy excluded applications:
https://github.com/mullvad/mullvadvpn-app/issues/10738

Do not enable this experimental feature on production-critical systems until
you have tested the driver version bundled with your build. If the backend
cannot start, FPTN continues with ordinary VPN routing instead.
